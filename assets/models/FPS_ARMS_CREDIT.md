# FPS viewmodel arms

- Model: **fps arms (rigged only)** by para, from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/fps-arms-rigged-only).
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**. The author dedicates the work to the public domain; attribution is appreciated but not required.
- Imported source: `_fps_arms.fbx` with its accompanying MakeHuman skin texture in `_para_skin.png`.
- `arm_para_l.res` and `arm_para_r.res` are the original skinned mesh split into left/right triangle subsets by `tools/model_pipeline/split_arms.gd`. The source rig, skin binds, and vertex data are retained.
